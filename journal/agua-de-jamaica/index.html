Agua de Jamaica, the drink that is always on the counter

There is always a pitcher of Agua de Jamaica on the counter at La Milpa. It is made from dried hibiscus flowers steeped in hot water, then sweetened with cane sugar, cooled, and finished with fresh lime.

The colour is a deep ruby red, and the flavour is tart, a little like cranberry, with a floral note underneath. It is a good match for something rich, like the Mole Negro con Pollo or the Camarones a la Diabla, because the tartness clears the palate between bites.

We make it fresh each morning and it is gone by evening on a hot day. If you would like something else, the Agua de Tamarindo and the Horchata are made the same way, and none of the three has any alcohol.
